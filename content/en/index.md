::landing-features
---
headline: Features
title: Everything you need to showcase your territory
---

  ::landing-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-search
  title: Advanced geographic search
  ---
  Find any point of interest by name, category or address. Explore areas reachable by bike, car or on foot with the isochrone feature.
  ::

  ::landing-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-database
  title: Multi-source territorial data
  ---
  Connect OpenStreetMap, your tourism information systems (Sirtaqui, Apidae, Tourinsoft), Geotrek, Open Agenda, GTFS or your own content managed via the back-office.
  ::

  ::landing-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-smartphone
  title: Designed for field use
  ---
  Full-screen responsive interface, optimized for mobile, tablet and desktop. Built for field agents, reception staff and visitors on the go.
  ::

  ::landing-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-bookmark
  title: Favorites and sharing
  ---
  Build favorite notebooks as lists or maps, and share them in one click via URL or QR code. Ideal for tourism advisors.
  ::

  ::landing-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-code-2
  title: Embeddable via widget
  ---
  Embed CartoGuide into any website in a few lines of code. An open API to connect your partner tools and customize every aspect of the interface.
  ::

  ::landing-feature
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-pencil
  title: Contributor mode
  ---
  Let your agents and volunteers enrich OpenStreetMap data directly from the map, without leaving the CartoGuide interface.
  ::
