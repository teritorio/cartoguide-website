  ::landing-use-case
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-compass
  title: Tourism & attractiveness
  ---
  Tourist offices, destinations and spa resorts — showcase accommodations, restaurants, activities and trails on a map enriched from your tourism information systems (Sirtaqui, Apidae, Tourinsoft). Favorites, QR code sharing and widget for partner websites.
  ::

  ::landing-use-case
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-building-2
  title: Local authorities
  ---
  Municipalities, inter-municipal communities and departments — keep residents informed about public services, businesses, facilities, mobility and local resources. INSEE, OSM and proprietary back-office data in a single map.
  ::

  ::landing-use-case
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-leaf
  title: Food & short supply chains
  ---
  Territories and pays — highlight local producers, markets and gourmet trails to support local consumption and short-circuit agriculture.
  ::

  ::landing-use-case
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-heart-pulse
  title: Healthcare
  ---
  Healthcare professional communities — make it easy for residents to find health professionals in your area with an interactive directory map, filterable by specialty, accessibility and care options.
  ::
