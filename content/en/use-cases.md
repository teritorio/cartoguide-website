  ::use-case-card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-compass
  title: Tourism & attractiveness
  screenshot: /use-cases/screenshot-tourisme.webp
  link: https://infoalacarte.fr
  linkLabel: See Grand Dax map
  ---
  Give your visitors every reason to stay longer. Tourist offices, destinations and spa resorts bring together accommodations, restaurants, activities and trails on a single map enriched from your tourism information systems (Sirtaqui, Apidae, Tourinsoft). Favorites, QR code sharing and embeddable widget for partners — everything you need to extend the stay.
  ::

  ::use-case-card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-building-2
  title: Local authorities
  screenshot: /use-cases/screenshot-collectivites.webp
  link: https://carte.mairie-begles.fr
  linkLabel: See Bègles map
  ---
  Bring residents closer to the services that matter to them. Municipalities, inter-municipal communities and departments publish their facilities, public services, businesses and local resources with a single click. Native connections to INSEE, OSM and your back-office data — one map, always up to date.
  ::

  ::use-case-card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-leaf
  title: Food & short supply chains
  screenshot: /use-cases/screenshot-alimentation.webp
  link: https://alimentation.pays-alo.fr/
  linkLabel: See Pays ALO map
  ---
  Connect local producers and consumers. Territories map their producers, markets, CSA farms and gourmet routes to boost local consumption and strengthen the territory's food resilience.
  ::

  ::use-case-card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-heart-pulse
  title: Healthcare
  screenshot: /use-cases/screenshot-sante.webp
  link: https://carte.boostupsante.fr
  linkLabel: See CPTS Boost'UP map
  ---
  Make healthcare accessible in your catchment area. Healthcare professional communities publish an interactive map directory of local health professionals, filterable by specialty, accessibility and care options — so every resident finds the right practitioner, in the right place.
  ::
